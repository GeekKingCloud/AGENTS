# Task Brief

Use only the sections that help preserve durable control state. The brief is not a required owner-facing form for every substantial task.

## Mode

implement / report-only / review / recovery / research.

## Target

Exact repo, path, file, branch, session, or artifact the work applies to.

## Source of truth

Exact branch, repo, raw source, or ticket that decides correctness. Installed copies, scratch dirs, and summaries are evidence only unless explicitly designated here.

## Boundary

Touch X, ignore Y, ask before Z.

## Deliverable shape

Guidance, implementation, diagnosis, evidence, or artifact; the least machinery needed by the intended operator.

## Autonomy

act / recommend / ask before mutation / stop at review / local-only / specifically authorized remote actions.

## Owner sequencing

Requested outputs in order; report-then-continue or report-then-wait; explicit stop/approval gates and any real delivery/resumption limitation. Planning alone does not authorize significant implementation or expansion; preserve already-granted steps.

## Review approach, if warranted

proportionate self-review / purposeful sub-agents with named roles / Roast / another required reviewer / no separate review needed.

## Complexity budget

Allowed new abstractions or infrastructure, broad verification runs, and review rounds. Default to no new machinery, one broad review when warranted, and one focused confirmation of repaired blockers unless the owner or nearest repository requires more. Selected procedures must stay inside this budget.

## Success check

Exact command, artifact, or state that proves the goal.

## Stop rule

Stop on material ambiguity, failed proof, long-running stall without new evidence, or a new authority requirement.

Checkpoint and begin a separately authorized next stage in fresh context after a material task-category or scope change, or when two repairs of the same mechanism produce no new evidence.
